Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy describes how the Vesper Logs publishing tool ("the App") handles information. The App is a personal tool operated by the owner of the Vesper Logs accounts. It is not offered to the public and is used only to manage the owner's own social media accounts.

Information the App accesses

When the account owner authorizes the App on a platform (such as TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, YouTube, X or Bluesky), the App receives an access token and basic account information (account ID, username and display name) needed to publish content to that account. The App may also read public engagement data on the owner's own posts, such as comments, likes and follower counts, in order to show them to the owner.

How the information is used

The information is used only to upload videos and captions created and approved by the account owner to the owner's own profiles, and to show the owner statistics and comments about their own posts. The App does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens and data are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never published. They can be revoked at any time from each platform's settings.

Third-party users

The App does not collect personal data from other users. Public comments on the owner's posts may be displayed to the owner for the purpose of replying; they are not stored beyond what is needed for that purpose.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any data related to the App, contact the owner at the address below. Revoking the App's access on the platform also stops all data access immediately.
